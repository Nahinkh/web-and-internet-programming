About the Event

What is SEU Tech Event?

SEU Tech Event is the flagship technology gathering organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southeast University. Now in its third year, the event aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world technology practice.

Our Goals

The primary objective of this event is to provide students with hands-on exposure to modern web technologies, foster a culture of collaborative learning, and prepare participants for careers in software development and IT.

Event Venue

All sessions will be held at the Department Auditorium and Computer Lab, Southeast University, Tejgaon, Dhaka.

Participation Details

Participation is open to all undergraduate students of Southeast University, regardless of department. Students from other universities may also attend as guests by registering in advance. There is no registration fee for SEU students. Certificates of participation will be issued to all attendees who complete at least three sessions.

Event Highlights

What to Expect

Two packed days of talks, workshops, and competitions covering the latest in web and software technology.

01

Key Topics

  • Modern HTML5 and semantic markup
  • CSS layout techniques and Flexbox
  • JavaScript fundamentals and DOM
  • Introduction to React framework
  • Version control with Git
02

Event Flow

  1. Opening ceremony and keynote
  2. Morning technical sessions
  3. Hands-on workshop labs
  4. Coding competition rounds
  5. Closing and certificate distribution
03

Who Should Attend

  • CSE students seeking practical skills
  • Beginners with no prior coding experience
  • Students preparing for software careers
  • Anyone curious about web technology

Detailed Session Breakdown

  • Day 1 — Foundations and Talks
    • Opening keynote by faculty advisor
    • HTML5 structure and semantic elements
    • CSS styling and responsive design basics
  • Day 2 — Workshops and Competition
    • JavaScript hands-on lab session
    • Build a complete webpage from scratch
    • Live coding competition with prizes
    • Certificate distribution and closing speech
